# OCBot Privacy Policy Last updated: November 14, 2026 ## 1. Purpose OCBot is a personal automation application used by its owner to access Google Workspace services through Google APIs. ## 2. Google User Data OCBot may access Google Drive, Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Sheets, and Google Docs data according to the permissions explicitly granted by the account owner. ## 3. Data Usage Google user data is used solely to perform the operations requested by the account owner, such as reading files, managing emails, accessing calendar events, and processing documents. ## 4. Data Storage OAuth credentials are stored locally on the owner's Debian VPS in encrypted form. Access tokens and refresh tokens are not intentionally shared with third parties. ## 5. Data Sharing OCBot does not sell, rent, or share Google user data with third parties for advertising or unrelated purposes. ## 6. Data Security Access to the VPS and OAuth credentials is restricted to the application owner. Appropriate access controls are used to protect stored credentials. ## 7. Data Retention and Deletion The account owner may revoke OCBot's Google account access at any time through their Google Account security settings and may delete locally stored application credentials. ## 8. Contact For privacy questions, contact the application owner through the Google account associated with this application.
